NomoPrivacy Policy

Delete your Nomo account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Nomo and go to Profile → Settings (the gear).
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Type your username to confirm, and tap Delete for good.

Without the app

Email findingteemo47@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, asking us to delete your account. We'll do it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What's deleted

Your account, profile, places, photos, lists, follows, likes, comments, saves and blocks are deleted straight away and can't be recovered. Backup copies of photos are removed within a day, and database backups within 14 days. Reports you made of other people's content are kept without your name. See the Privacy Policy for details.
