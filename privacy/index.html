Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

Draft. This policy hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer yet and may change before Nomo is publicly available. It's written with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA) in mind.

Nomo ("we", "us") is a food journal: you rate the places you eat at, keep notes and photos, and share them with people you choose. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who handles it for us, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and a password (stored only in scrambled form), or your Google account's email if you sign in with Google. Your username, bio, whether your account is public or private, your persona and rating standards, and your notification choices.
  • What you post: the places you log, their ratings, notes, visit dates and counts, your lists, your photos, and your likes, comments, saves, follows and blocks.
  • Photos: we remove all hidden information from photos on your phone before they're uploaded, including location (GPS), camera details and the time taken.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, a token that lets us send them to your phone.
  • Reports: if you report something, what you reported and why.
  • Location: a future version may let you pin a place on a map. That will be optional and only used for the places you choose to pin.

We don't collect your contacts, track you across other apps or websites, or use analytics or advertising trackers. We don't sell your personal data.

Why we use it

  • To run your account and keep your journal, on your phone and on our servers.
  • To show your profile and places to others according to your settings: anyone signed in can see a public account's places; only the followers you accept can see a private account's.
  • To send the notifications you've switched on.
  • To keep Nomo safe: blocking, and reviewing reports of content that breaks our Terms.
  • To keep backups, so your journal can be restored if something goes wrong.

We only use your personal data for these purposes, which you consent to by creating an account.

Who handles it for us

We use these providers to run Nomo. They handle data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in and photo storage, hosted in Singapore [confirm region], and a second Supabase project that keeps a backup copy of photos.
  • GitHub: stores daily database backups in a private place for 14 days.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivers notifications to your phone. We don't use other Firebase services or Google Analytics.
  • Google Sign-In, only if you choose to sign in with Google.

Some of these providers may process data outside Singapore. We only use providers that protect personal data to a standard comparable to the PDPA.

If we turn on ads in future, we'll update this policy first and tell you in the app.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and everything in it, for as long as you have the account.
  • When you delete your account (Settings → Delete account), we delete it immediately: your profile, places, photos, lists, follows, likes and comments. Backup copies of photos are removed within a day; database backups are removed as they age out, within 14 days.
  • A record that an account was deleted (only its internal number and the date), so its photo backups can be removed.
  • Reports you made stay after you delete your account, without your name. Reports about you or your posts are deleted with your account.
  • A log of notifications sent, kept for 30 days to avoid sending too many.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your data: most of it is in the app, where you can edit or delete it. To ask for a copy of your personal data or for corrections, email us.
  • Control who sees you: make your account private, remove followers, or block people.
  • Notifications: switch each kind on or off in Settings.
  • Withdraw consent: delete your account in Settings, or email us. Without consent we can't run your account, so withdrawing it means your account is deleted.

Children

Nomo is for people aged 13 and over. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data travels encrypted between the app and our servers. Our database only lets each person reach what they're allowed to see, and we test those rules automatically. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal data, we'll tell you and the Personal Data Protection Commission as the PDPA requires.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before it applies.

Contact

Questions, requests or complaints, including to our Data Protection Officer: findingteemo47@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 30 days.
